Websites for yoga studios, gyms and fitness classes

Nobody commits to a year of anything from a website. Knowing that changes what the site should be.

The conversion is the trial class, not the membership

Someone on your site is not deciding whether to join. They are deciding whether to turn up once. Those are completely different questions, and a site built to sell annual memberships answers neither.

So the trial is the primary action on every page, and the schedule has to answer two things in the first screen: when are the classes, and which one should a beginner book?

What we have learned building these

The things that decide whether a site in this trade works, which are rarely the things a brochure talks about.

  • 01

    A beginner does not know which class to pick

    A schedule listing Ashtanga, Vinyasa and Iyengar means nothing to someone who has never been. Say which one to start with, in words, and the trial booking doubles.

  • 02

    The timetable is the most-visited page

    Existing members check it constantly. If it is a photograph of a printed sheet, they will ask you on WhatsApp instead, forever.

  • 03

    Teacher credentials carry more weight than the room

    Where they trained, how long they have taught, what they specialise in. People are choosing a person, not a floor.

  • 04

    Health claims are a trap

    Curing conditions, guaranteed weight loss, therapeutic promises — all of it is risk, and none of it is needed. Specific beats miraculous.

Health and outcome claims

Claims about curing conditions or guaranteed results are both a legal risk and, in practice, less persuasive than a clear schedule and an honest description of a beginner class. We keep the copy on the side of what you actually do.

Questions we get from this trade

Our schedule changes every month. Is that a problem?
No, it is the normal case. The schedule is built to be edited — send the new timetable and it is updated, or we can put you on a simple CMS if you would rather do it yourself. What does not work is a photo of a printed sheet.
Can people pay for memberships on the site?
Yes, but consider whether you want to. Most studios convert better by getting someone into a free or cheap trial and selling the membership in person. If you do want online payments, that is the store build with a payment gateway in your name.
We run a gym, not a yoga studio. Does this still apply?
Almost entirely. Gyms, pilates, dance schools, martial arts and coaching centres all sell the same thing: a schedule, a first session, and the person teaching it. The template is the same and the copy changes.
