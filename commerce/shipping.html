Shipping policy

This is a demonstration store, so these policies describe a business that does not trade. They are written as real policies rather than placeholder text because Razorpay will not activate a live account without them, and because a customer who reads one deserves a straight answer.

Where we ship

Anywhere in India. We do not ship internationally at present, because handicraft export paperwork is a different business from retail and we would rather do one of them properly.

Dispatch time

Orders placed before 2 PM on a working day are dispatched the next working day. Anything ordered on Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday goes out on the next working day after that.

Two exceptions, both honest ones:

  • Made-to-order pieces — anything marked as such is woven or cast after you order it. The product page gives the real lead time, which is usually three to six weeks.
  • Single-stock pieces — if the last one is damaged in packing we will tell you the same day and refund in full rather than make you wait for a replacement that may not exist.

Charges

Delivery is free on orders over ₹2,500. Below that it is a flat ₹150 anywhere in India. The charge is shown in the basket before you pay, never added afterwards.

Tracking

You get a tracking link by email and WhatsApp when the parcel leaves us. If the tracking has not moved for three working days, write to us and we will chase the courier — you should not have to.

If it arrives damaged

Terracotta and glass travel badly, which is why we double-box them. If something arrives broken, photograph it before unpacking any further and send us the photograph within 48 hours. We will replace it or refund it in full, including the delivery charge. We do not ask you to post broken pottery back to us.

Wrong or missing address

If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incomplete or nobody was available across three delivery attempts, we will refund the order less the actual return freight. We will always contact you before that point.
