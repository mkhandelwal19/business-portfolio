Refunds and cancellation

This is a demonstration store, so these policies describe a business that does not trade. They are written as real policies rather than placeholder text because Razorpay will not activate a live account without them, and because a customer who reads one deserves a straight answer.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel free of charge at any point before the parcel is dispatched. Email or WhatsApp us with the order reference and the refund starts the same day.

Once it has been handed to the courier it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse delivery or return it — see below.

Returns

Seven days from delivery, for any reason at all, provided the piece is unused and in the condition it arrived in. You do not have to justify it to us.

What cannot be returned, and why:

  • Made-to-order and customised pieces — a saree woven to your colour cannot be resold to anyone else.
  • Pierced jewellery — hygiene.
  • Anything used, washed or altered — including sarees that have had fall and pico stitched, since that is a permanent change.

How to return something

  1. Write to us within seven days of delivery with the order reference.
  2. We arrange a reverse pickup where the courier serves your PIN code. Where they do not, we reimburse your postage against a receipt.
  3. Pack it as it arrived. Handicraft breaks in transit and a broken return is a harder conversation for both of us.

We process the refund within 48 hours of the returned parcel reaching us. The rest of that time is the bank's, and neither of us can hurry it. Refunds go back to the original payment method — we cannot send it somewhere else, and a shop that offers to is a shop to be careful of.

Faulty or wrong items

Our mistake, our cost. Full refund including both delivery charges, or a replacement, whichever you prefer. No seven-day limit applies to a genuine fault.

What "faulty" does not mean

Handmade pieces vary. A Bankura horse fired in an open kiln will not be the exact colour of the photograph; a Dhokra casting carries the wax thread marks of the person who wound it. That variation is the object, not a defect — and if a piece arrived uniform and identical it would be a sign that a machine made it.

If the variation is not what you wanted, that is a perfectly good reason to return it under the seven-day policy above. We just will not call it a fault.
